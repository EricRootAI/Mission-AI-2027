# Consulting Case Study Matrix

This document maps the existing Mission AI 2027 portfolio to the consulting capabilities it demonstrates and identifies the highest-value evidence gaps that should be addressed next.

The objective is not to create more portfolio material for its own sake.

The objective is to ensure that every major project demonstrates a capability that supports Eric Root AI's consulting positioning and moves the portfolio closer to professional and commercial readiness.

# 7. Highest-Value Evidence Gap

## Gap

**Demonstrating measurable business value from an AI transformation initiative.**

The current portfolio shows that Eric can identify problems, design AI opportunities, develop documentation, consider responsible AI, and create practical solutions.

The next major credibility step is demonstrating how an AI initiative could be evaluated in terms of:

- Business value
- Operational improvement
- Time savings
- Cost reduction
- Employee impact
- Customer impact
- Risk reduction
- Adoption
- Return on investment

## Why It Matters

Recruiters and potential clients ultimately need to know more than whether someone understands AI.

They need confidence that the consultant can connect AI activity to organizational outcomes.

A strong business-value demonstration would bridge the gap between:

> **"I can design an AI solution."**

and:

> **"I can help an organization determine whether the solution is worth implementing."**

That distinction is central to Eric Root AI's consulting positioning.

## Proposed Future Artifact

**AI Transformation Business Case & ROI Framework**

The artifact should demonstrate how a proposed AI initiative can be evaluated from an executive and operational perspective before significant implementation resources are committed.

It should include:

- Business problem
- Current-state process
- AI opportunity
- Proposed solution
- Expected operational impact
- Expected human impact
- Cost considerations
- Benefit assumptions
- ROI methodology
- Risk considerations
- Success metrics
- Implementation phases
- Executive recommendation

## Priority

**HIGH**

# 8. Strategic Direction

The next phase of Mission AI 2027 should not focus primarily on creating more AI demonstrations.

The priority should be strengthening the connection between existing demonstrations and **real consulting decision-making**.

The portfolio should increasingly demonstrate the ability to move an organization through:

**Discover → Diagnose → Design → Demonstrate → Deploy → Measure**

The next major build should therefore strengthen the **Measure** component of this framework.

---

# 9. Mission AI 2027 Portfolio Standard

Before adding a new major portfolio artifact, evaluate it against four questions:

### 1. Does it demonstrate a consulting capability?

### 2. Does it strengthen one of the three professional positions?

### 3. Does it fill a meaningful evidence gap?

### 4. Does it move the portfolio closer to professional or commercial readiness?

If the answer to all four is not clear, the artifact should not be considered a priority.
