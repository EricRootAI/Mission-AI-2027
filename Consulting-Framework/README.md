# AI Consulting Framework

This folder contains reusable frameworks developed as part of **Mission AI 2027** for evaluating, designing, implementing, and governing practical AI initiatives.

The focus is business-first AI adoption: identifying real operational problems, determining whether AI is an appropriate solution, defining measurable value, managing risk, and maintaining appropriate human oversight.

## Frameworks

### [AI Consulting Capability Framework](./AI-Consulting-Capability-Framework.md)

A cross-industry consulting framework covering the core capabilities required to guide organizations through practical AI adoption.

Key areas include:

- AI strategy and organizational readiness
- Workflow automation
- Knowledge and document intelligence
- AI-assisted decision support
- Customer and employee experience
- Responsible AI governance
- Implementation planning and adoption
- Measurement, ROI, and continuous improvement

This framework represents the broader consulting methodology used to move from business need to responsible AI implementation.

---

### [AI Sales Discovery & POC Framework](./AI_Sales_Discovery_POC_Framework.md)

A structured framework for moving an enterprise AI opportunity from initial customer discovery through a controlled proof of concept and evidence-based deployment decision.

The framework covers:

1. Business Discovery
2. AI Use-Case Qualification
3. Stakeholder & Risk Assessment
4. POC Design
5. Success Metrics & Evaluation
6. Go / No-Go / Scale Decision

The approach emphasizes identifying measurable business value before recommending AI, validating data and system readiness, defining human escalation paths, limiting initial POC scope, and evaluating results before expansion.

## Core Principle

> AI should not be implemented simply because it is technically possible. A successful AI initiative should solve a meaningful business problem, operate within clearly defined boundaries, produce measurable value, and preserve appropriate human oversight.
