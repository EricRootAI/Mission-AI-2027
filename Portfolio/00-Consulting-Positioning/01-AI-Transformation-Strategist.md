# AI Transformation Strategist

## Position

I help organizations identify where artificial intelligence can create meaningful business value—and where it cannot.

My focus is translating organizational challenges into practical AI opportunities, prioritizing initiatives according to business impact, feasibility, risk, and organizational readiness.

I work from the problem backward rather than the technology forward.

## Questions I Help Organizations Answer

- Where can AI create measurable value?
- Which problems are actually worth solving with AI?
- What should be automated, augmented, or left human-led?
- What risks must be addressed before implementation?
- What would successful adoption look like?
- How should an organization move from experimentation to sustainable use?

## Approach

My approach begins with understanding the organization, its objectives, its operational constraints, and the people affected by potential AI implementation.

From there, I identify opportunities, evaluate feasibility and risk, prioritize initiatives, and develop practical transformation roadmaps.

## Core Value

**Turning AI possibility into strategic direction.**

## Guiding Principle

> **The right AI solution begins with the right business problem.**
