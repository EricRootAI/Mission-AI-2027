# Why Eric?

Artificial intelligence is changing faster than most organizations can comfortably absorb.

The challenge isn't simply learning what AI can do.

The challenge is understanding where it should be used, how it should be implemented, and how it can create value without losing sight of the people who use it.

My perspective comes from the intersection of operational leadership, hospitality, customer experience, and artificial intelligence.

That combination shapes how I approach consulting.

I don't begin with:

> "What AI tool should we use?"

I begin with:

> **"What problem are we trying to solve, and what would better look like?"**

From there, I work backward toward the technology.

That means evaluating the process, identifying the friction, determining whether AI is actually appropriate, designing the solution, considering responsible-AI requirements, and defining how the organization will know whether the solution worked.

## The Difference

I don't believe every problem needs AI.

I believe organizations need better judgment about **where AI belongs**.

That means knowing when to:

- Automate
- Augment
- Analyze
- Assist
- Escalate to humans
- Or leave a process entirely human

## Core Philosophy

**AI is the capability.**

**Transformation is the objective.**

**Human and business impact is the measure of success.**
