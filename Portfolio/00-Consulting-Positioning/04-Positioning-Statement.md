# Eric Root AI — Positioning Statement

## Primary Positioning

**I help organizations identify where AI can create meaningful operational value, design practical and responsible solutions, and build the roadmap for turning those opportunities into measurable results.**

## Short Version

**I help organizations put AI where it matters.**

## Conversational Version

I help organizations figure out where AI can actually make a difference—not just where it is technically possible.

I start with the business problem, identify the opportunity, design the solution, consider the risks, and connect the implementation to measurable outcomes.

## Positioning Principle

> **Problem first. Technology second. Impact always.**
