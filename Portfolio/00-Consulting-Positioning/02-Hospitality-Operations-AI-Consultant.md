# Hospitality & Operations AI Consultant

## Position

I help hospitality and customer-centric organizations identify practical opportunities to use AI to improve operations, employee experiences, customer experiences, and decision-making.

My approach begins with the realities of the operation: the people, processes, constraints, service expectations, and business objectives.

I look for the friction points that consume time, create inconsistency, limit visibility, or prevent employees and leaders from focusing on higher-value work.

## AI Opportunity Areas

I evaluate where AI can:

- Reduce repetitive operational work
- Improve access to organizational knowledge
- Strengthen employee onboarding and development
- Improve consistency in processes and service delivery
- Surface insights from operational information
- Support better management decisions
- Enhance customer experiences
- Create scalable operational capabilities

## Consulting Philosophy

The goal is not to replace the human element that makes hospitality work.

**The goal is to give people better tools to do the work that matters most.**

## Approach

I begin with the operation rather than the technology.

That means understanding:

- What employees actually do
- Where time is being lost
- Where processes break down
- Where information becomes difficult to access
- Where customers experience friction
- Where managers lack visibility
- Where AI could create measurable improvement

Only then do I determine whether AI is an appropriate solution.

## Core Value

**Applying AI where operational experience meets human experience.**
