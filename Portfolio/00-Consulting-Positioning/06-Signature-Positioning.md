# Signature Positioning

## The Eric Root AI Principle

> **I don't put AI everywhere. I put AI where it matters.**

This principle defines my approach to AI consulting.

Artificial intelligence should not be adopted simply because it is new, impressive, or available.

It should be applied when it can create meaningful value for an organization and the people it serves.

That requires asking better questions before selecting technology.

### The Questions

1. What problem are we actually trying to solve?
2. Who is affected by the problem?
3. What would meaningful improvement look like?
4. Is AI actually the right solution?
5. What should AI do—and what should humans continue to do?
6. What risks must be addressed?
7. How will the organization measure success?

### The Standard

A successful AI initiative is not defined by how sophisticated the technology appears.

It is defined by whether the solution:

- Solves a real problem
- Improves the experience of the people involved
- Creates measurable value
- Can be responsibly implemented
- Can be maintained and improved over time

**Put AI where it matters.**
