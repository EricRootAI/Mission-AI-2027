Business Problem Analysis

Purpose

This document defines the healthcare business challenge addressed by the AI-Powered Hospital Readmission Prediction & Care Intervention System.

The objective is to understand the problem from a consulting perspective before selecting datasets, building predictive models, or implementing artificial intelligence solutions.

⸻

Business Challenge

Hospital readmissions remain a significant concern for healthcare organizations because they can increase costs, place additional demands on clinical resources, and negatively affect patient outcomes.

Healthcare providers need better ways to identify patients who may be at elevated risk of returning to the hospital shortly after discharge so that appropriate interventions can be planned in advance.

Traditional methods often rely on manual review and clinical experience, which can be difficult to apply consistently across large patient populations.

⸻

Why AI?

Artificial intelligence can assist healthcare organizations by identifying patterns within patient data that may not be immediately apparent through manual analysis.

Rather than replacing clinical judgment, AI serves as a decision-support tool that helps care teams prioritize patients who may benefit from additional follow-up, education, or care coordination.

⸻

Business Objectives

This project aims to:

* Improve identification of patients at higher risk of readmission.
* Support proactive care planning.
* Assist healthcare teams with data-informed decision making.
* Demonstrate responsible AI implementation in a regulated industry.
* Illustrate how AI can create measurable business value while supporting patient care.

⸻

Success Measures

Potential measures of success include:

* Improved identification of high-risk patients.
* Better allocation of clinical resources.
* Reduced preventable readmissions.
* Increased transparency through explainable AI.
* Responsible and ethical deployment of AI-assisted decision support.

⸻

Consulting Perspective

This project is presented as a consulting engagement rather than simply a machine learning exercise.

The emphasis is on understanding the business problem, designing an appropriate AI solution, considering responsible AI principles, and communicating measurable organizational value.
