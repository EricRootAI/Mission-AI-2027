AI-Powered Hospital Readmission Prediction & Care Intervention System

Executive Summary

This portfolio project demonstrates the design of an AI-powered healthcare solution that predicts the risk of hospital readmission and supports proactive patient care interventions.

The goal of this project is to showcase how artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, and responsible AI practices can help healthcare organizations identify high-risk patients earlier, improve care coordination, and make data-informed decisions.

This project is designed from a healthcare AI consulting perspective, focusing not only on model development but also on business value, ethical implementation, and practical deployment considerations.

⸻

Business Challenge

Hospital readmissions create significant challenges for healthcare organizations, including:

* Increased healthcare costs
* Strain on clinical resources
* Reduced operational efficiency
* Difficulty identifying high-risk patients before discharge

Healthcare teams need solutions that help prioritize patients who may benefit from additional support, follow-up care, and intervention.

⸻
