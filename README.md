## 🏥 Healthcare — AI Readmission Prevention

The healthcare portfolio explores an AI-assisted system designed to identify patients who may be at elevated risk of hospital readmission.

The work includes:

- Business problem definition
- Synthetic-data modeling
- Logistic regression
- Threshold optimization
- Risk-tier design
- Model evaluation
- Explainability considerations
- Human oversight
- Workflow integration
- Governance and deployment controls

### Prototype Results

The educational prototype produced:

- **72.0% test accuracy**
- **0.25 F1-optimized threshold**
- **47.5% precision**
- **85.1% recall**
- **61.0% F1 score**
- **71.1% ± 1.6% five-fold cross-validation accuracy**
- Approximately **3.76× higher observed readmission rate** in the high-risk group than the low-risk group

The project is explicitly presented as an educational prototype and **not as a clinically deployable medical system**.

That distinction is intentional.

A model that produces useful predictions is not automatically safe, appropriate, or ready for production deployment—particularly in a high-impact environment such as healthcare.

## ❄️ Summit Climate Systems — AI Service Operations

The Summit Climate Systems case study expands Mission AI 2027 into field service and HVAC-style operations.

The project explores how an AI-assisted intake and dispatch environment could improve service response while preserving human control over consequential decisions.

Current design areas include:

- Structured customer-request intake
- Missing-information detection
- CRM data verification
- Stale-data safeguards
- P1–P4 urgency classification
- Time-based escalation
- Human dispatcher review
- Customer communication
- Human-in-the-loop operational control

A core design principle is:

> **AI should detect, summarize, recommend, and alert. Humans should verify, decide, communicate, and override when necessary.**

➡️ [AI Intake, Verification & Escalation Design](Portfolio/Portfolio-09-Summit-Climate-Systems-AI-Intake-and-Escalation.md)

Future Summit work will expand into technician routing, capacity management, skill matching, geographic assignment, and resource-constrained dispatch decisions.

# 💡 My Approach

I do not believe every business problem requires AI.

Sometimes AI will be the right solution.

Sometimes traditional automation will be enough.

Sometimes the responsible recommendation will be to improve the existing process without introducing additional technology.

And sometimes the correct answer will be **NO-GO**.

My goal is not to sell AI for the sake of AI.

My goal is to:

**Understand the problem.  
Evaluate the evidence.  
Identify the risks.  
Design appropriate human oversight.  
Recommend the right solution.  
Measure the outcome.**
